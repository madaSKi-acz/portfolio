01 — About

Solving problems, not repeating tasks.

I'm a software engineer with almost four years of experience. I'm at my best when there's a real problem to solve, like a slow workflow, a process people do by hand, or a system that no longer fits how the business works. If I have to do the same task twice, I start looking for a way to automate it.

Most of my work is on the front end, where I build interfaces with Vue, Pinia and Tailwind. I also know enough back end to work across the whole stack. I've built features with Laravel, Node.js and Python, designed and analyzed databases, and built mapping features with OpenStreetMap and Google Maps.

I learn quickly and I'm comfortable switching tools when a project calls for it. I work closely with AI agents, using them to explore ideas, generate scaffolding and speed up delivery, while I stay responsible for the architecture and the quality of the result.

02 — Approach

How I work

Problem first

I start with the business problem before picking tools. I want to know who is slowed down, what it costs, and what "better" actually looks like.

Automate the repeat

When I see a repetitive task, I treat it as something to fix. I'd rather build a tool once than do the same job by hand every week.

Adapt quickly

New framework, new domain, new team: I pick things up fast and I don't mind working outside my usual area.

AI-augmented

I use AI agents as part of how I build. They help me prototype, refactor and test faster, and I keep the judgment and code review on my side.
